Holozoa

Approximate age: ~1.1–0.9 Ga; uncertain molecular-clock range

Environment: Aquatic microbial food webs with variable oxygen and nutrient supplies; many relatives were unicellular or formed simple collectives.

Major innovations: Adhesion, signaling and cell-state regulation that predated animals and were later recruited into animal multicellularity.
