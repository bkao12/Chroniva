Proposed prebiotic chemistry

Approximate age: ~4.4–4.0 Ga; candidate interval, not a dated origin

Environment: Young Earth: volcanic and hydrothermal chemistry, mineral surfaces and possible wet–dry cycles; little free oxygen and no modern ozone shield.

Major innovations: Candidate building-block synthesis, chemical energy coupling and molecular compartments; these are hypotheses, not established living systems.

Context sources: early life

Read each broad period through functional sets, useful sub-aspects and canonical BP mechanisms. The five personal abstractions are navigation lenses only; they do not assert containment, ancestry or a common mechanism.

Placements are broad working interpretations. “Provisional” marks candidate or reconstructed placements. Present-day anatomy is a learning reference, not evidence of ancient organs or machinery. Candidate prebiotic reactions do not establish whole-system self-maintenance or historical occurrence.

Bracket counts describe only the selected linked entries. “Part of” means mechanistic participation; “regulates” and “enables” express different relationships. Alternative mechanisms depend on context: homologous recombination and end joining apply to different repair situations; cytosolic and vacuolar cross-presentation are different antigen-processing routes.
