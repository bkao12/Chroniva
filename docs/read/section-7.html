Approximate age: From ~1.8–1.6 Ga onward; multiple comparison lineages, not one origin

Environment: Marine, freshwater, terrestrial and host-associated habitats; exposure varies greatly among plants, fungi and protists.

Major innovations: Independent specializations such as plastid-based photosynthesis, cell walls, multicellularity and parasitic adaptations; not one shared package.
