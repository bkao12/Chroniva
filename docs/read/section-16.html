Tetrapoda

Approximate age: ~395–360 mya for early limbed tetrapod evolution; crown dates remain debated

Environment: Shallow water, wetlands and increasingly used terrestrial margins; variable oxygen, buoyancy and desiccation constraints.

Major innovations: Digit-bearing limbs, strengthened weight-bearing structures and reorganized locomotion; early tetrapods remained strongly aquatic.

Context sources: tetrapod/amniote evidence
