Hominoidea

Approximate age: ~30–20 mya for stem ape divergence; later crown-ape diversification

Environment: Oligocene–Miocene African woodlands and forests, later extending into Eurasian habitats with changing seasonality.

Major innovations: Ape-lineage body-plan changes including tail loss and later trunk/shoulder specializations; suspension and large brains were not an original universal package.

Context sources: ape divergence

Functional set: Reproduction and early embryonic development

Build maternal–embryonic interfaces

BP - physiology: Syncytin-1–ASCT2-dependent trophoblast cell fusion <thymus, lymph nodes, spleen, peripheral tissues;lymphoid tissue; T lymphocyte, thymic epithelial cell, antigen-presenting cell>
