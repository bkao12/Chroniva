Primates

Approximate age: ~70–55 mya for broad early primate evolution; clear euprimate fossils by ~56–55 mya

Environment: Predominantly wooded environments; branch-based locomotion and visually guided feeding in warm early Paleogene settings.

Major innovations: Grasping extremities, enhanced visual coordination and flexible arboreal behavior; individual traits have different evolutionary histories.

Context sources: early primates
