Approximate age: ~225–180 mya; mammaliaform-to-crown transition, definition-dependent

Environment: Mesozoic terrestrial ecosystems; many early forms were small and used low-light niches, with diverse later habitats.

Major innovations: Lactation, hair, specialized teeth and middle-ear remodeling assembled progressively; physiological traits did not all appear at one instant.
