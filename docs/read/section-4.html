Archaea

Approximate age: Early divergence after LUCA, broadly ~4.2–3.5 Ga; exact origin unresolved

Environment: Anoxic marine and sedimentary habitats; some lineages later occupied hot, saline or other extreme environments.

Major innovations: Distinctive ether-linked membranes and archaeal information-processing systems; methanogenesis in selected lineages, not all archaea.

Context sources: early life
