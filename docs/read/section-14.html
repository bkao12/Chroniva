Jawless vertebrates

Approximate age: ~520–480 mya for early jawless vertebrate grades; living jawless lineages have their own divergence dates

Environment: Early marine benthic and swimming niches; jawless feeding and variable body armor in extinct groups.

Major innovations: Early vertebrate feeding and sensory specializations; VLR-based adaptive immunity characterizes living cyclostomes, not every ancient jawless form.

Context sources: vertebrate origins

Functional set: Defense and immune coordination

Develop, activate and regulate adaptive immunity
