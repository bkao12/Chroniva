LUCA

Approximate age: ~4.2–3.5 Ga; highly model-dependent, with one recent estimate near 4.2 Ga

Environment: Largely anoxic early oceans; redox gradients and microbial interactions; precise habitat remains disputed.

Major innovations: Shared genetic code, ribosomal translation, cellular energy conservation and core biosynthesis; LUCA was not the first life.

Context sources: early life
