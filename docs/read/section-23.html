Homo sapiens

Approximate age: ~0.3 mya to present

Environment: Variable African climates and habitats, followed by worldwide dispersal and increasingly human-modified environments.

Major innovations: A mosaic of modern human anatomy and expanded symbolic/cumulative cultural capacities; upright walking, tool use and sociality predate Homo sapiens.

Context sources: human origins
