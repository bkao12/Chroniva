Bacteria

Approximate age: Early divergence after LUCA, broadly ~4.2–3.5 Ga; exact origin unresolved

Environment: Initially anoxic water and sediments; later expansion into illuminated, oxygenated and host-associated niches.

Major innovations: Bacterial envelope organization and metabolic diversification; oxygenic photosynthesis arose in particular later bacterial lineages.

Context sources: early life; oxygen history
