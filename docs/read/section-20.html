Eutheria

Approximate age: ~160–125 mya for stem eutherians; crown placental radiation broadly ~100–66 mya

Environment: Jurassic–Cretaceous terrestrial ecosystems, followed by major ecological opportunities after the end-Cretaceous extinction.

Major innovations: Eutherian reproductive and placental specializations supporting prolonged gestation in many descendants; distinguish stem eutherians from crown Placentalia.

Context sources: therian/eutherian evidence; mammal timescales

Functional set: Gene expression and RNA processing

Organize chromatin and regulate accessibility

BP - embryology / biochemistry: Eutherian XIST/XIC-associated X-chromosome silencing <multiple organs;proliferating and differentiated tissues; nucleated human cell> [2 components: ATP-dependent chromatin remodeling, DNA methylation and active demethylation]

Functional set: Defense and immune coordination

Develop, activate and regulate adaptive immunity

BP - immunology / physiology / biochemistry: Eutherian FcRn-dependent transplacental maternal IgG transfer <placenta; chorionic villus, maternal-fetal interface; syncytiotrophoblast, fetal endothelial cell>

Embryology / placenta: Chorionic villus; Chorion frondosum; Chorion laeve; Decidua basalis; Placental barrier; Maternal intervillous space; Fetal capillary; Placental cotyledon; Amnion; Chorion; Allantoic remnant; Human chorionic gonadotropin (hCG); Human placental lactogen (hPL)
