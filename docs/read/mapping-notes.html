Organization notes and source checks

Core microbial cellular biology remains chronological. Pathogen inventories and disease mechanisms are organized by affected host functions. Broad or mechanical harm stays in General Pathogens/Parasites, at the former virus-section position. Its brackets link mapped BP entries; mechanical, vector-borne, nutritional and diffuse tissue effects are named explicitly. Antimicrobial facts that are shared across organisms remain in the Microbiology/Parasitology reference subsections there.

Existing numerical network relationships are retained as biochemical context, not reinterpreted as counts of infections. Original stable BP destinations and named bracket links remain navigable; a link to a moved pathogen mechanism now reaches its blue mechanism entry.
